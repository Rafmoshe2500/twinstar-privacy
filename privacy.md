# Twinstar: Mirror Comets — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-04

Twinstar: Mirror Comets is published by Twinstar: Mirror Comets Games, available on Android and iOS (package/bundle id `com.twinstar.app` on both). The core game is offline by default and we operate no game servers that store your personal data. Optional features — online 1v1 multiplayer and cloud save — rely on Google and a network-relay provider, described below.

## Data we collect

- **Google Play Games Services (Android — leaderboards & cloud save)** — if you sign in (optional), Google processes your Play Games player ID, gamer profile/display name, submitted scores, and your saved game progress (Saved Games / Snapshots). Handled by Google, not by us.
- **Apple Game Center (iOS — leaderboards)** — if you choose to sign in, Apple processes your Game Center player ID, display name, and submitted scores, to provide leaderboards. Handled by Apple, not by us. Game Center is used for leaderboards only — on iOS your game progress is not backed up to the cloud (see "Game progress and responsibility" below). Signing in is optional and the game is fully playable without it.
- **Advertising ID / IDFA** — used by Google AdMob to show ads and (with your consent) personalize them. This is the Advertising ID on Android (reset or opt out in your device's settings → Google → Ads) and the IDFA on iOS (see "App Tracking Transparency" below).
- **Crash reports** — anonymous crash data collected by Google Play Console (Android) or Apple App Store Connect (iOS).
- **Online multiplayer (IP address)** — during an online 1v1 match, your device connects directly to your opponent's via WebRTC. Your public IP address and network candidate info are exchanged with the other player and processed by connection-helper servers (Google STUN and a third-party TURN relay provider, such as Open Relay or ExpressTURN). Firebase coordinates only the initial handshake (a temporary anonymous session), which is deleted on connect. No score/purchase/account data is involved, and nothing is stored on our servers.

## App Tracking Transparency (iOS)

On iOS, the game asks your permission before Google AdMob can use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. This is Apple's standard App Tracking Transparency prompt.

- If you **allow** tracking, AdMob may use the IDFA to personalize the ads you see and to measure ad performance.
- If you **ask the app not to track**, no IDFA is used for tracking. You still see ads, but they are not personalized. Nothing else in the game changes.
- You can change your choice at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

The tracking is performed by Google AdMob, not by us: we operate no advertising or analytics infrastructure of our own, we never receive or store tracking data, and we never sell it.

## Data we do not collect

Names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise (GPS) location, photos, contacts, microphone or camera input, or similar PII. We do not receive your Google account email or password, nor your Apple ID credentials. The only network identifier involved is your IP address, used solely to establish an online match and not stored by us.

## Game progress and responsibility

Your in-game progress — stars, scores, and unlocked or purchased skins — is stored on your device. On Android, when you are signed in to Google Play Games it is also backed up to your Google account via Google Play Games "Saved Games" (Snapshots), provided and controlled entirely by Google Play Games; we strongly recommend signing in so your progress is backed up. On iOS, this version stores progress on your device only — there is no cloud backup, so deleting the app or switching devices will lose your progress. We do not run our own progress servers. Because saving and syncing (where available) depend on your platform's services, your device, and (on Android) your Google account, we cannot guarantee progress will never be lost, and to the maximum extent permitted by law we accept no responsibility or liability for any loss, reset, or corruption of stars, scores, skins, or other in-game progress (including from reinstalling the game, switching or resetting devices, signing out of Google Play Games on Android, clearing app data, or interruptions to platform services). The game is provided "as is", without warranty.
