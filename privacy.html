Twinstar

Privacy Policy

Last updated · May 25, 2026

Twinstar is a mobile game published by Twinstar Games. The game is designed to be playable fully offline and stores no personal data on our own servers. This page describes the limited categories of data processed by third-party services we integrate with, what they are used for, and how to opt out.

Data we collect

  • Advertising ID — a resettable, device-level identifier provided by your operating system. It is used by Google AdMob to serve advertisements inside the game and, with your explicit consent, to personalize those advertisements. You can reset this identifier or opt out of personalized advertising at any time from your device settings (on Android: Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Crash and performance reports — when the game crashes or behaves abnormally, anonymous diagnostic data (device model, OS version, stack traces) may be sent to Google Play Console. We use this data exclusively to improve game stability. It cannot be used to identify you.

Data we do not collect

We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photos, contacts, microphone or camera input, or any other personally identifiable information. The game does not require an account and is fully functional offline (advertisements aside).

Third-party services

  • Google AdMob — serves advertisements inside the game. See Google's ad technologies policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — presents a consent form for personalized advertising in regions where required (EU, EEA, UK, Switzerland, and others). Your choice is stored on-device and respected by AdMob.
  • Google Play Services — provides crash reporting and game distribution infrastructure. See Google's privacy policy.

Children

Twinstar is not directed at children under the age of 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect or process data from children. If you believe a child has provided us with information through one of the third-party services above, please contact us and we will work with the relevant service to address it.

Data retention and deletion

Because we do not store user data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to retain or delete on your behalf. To remove all game data from your device, uninstall the application. To reset the Advertising ID, use your device settings as described above. For data held by Google (AdMob, Play Services), please refer to Google's data & privacy controls.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to how the game handles data, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
