What's new in 2.0

Four releases, one new game

Twinstar 2.0 arrived in four steps. Each one rebuilt part of the game, from the first minute on a new phone to four brand-new worlds.

R1

A fair first minute

  • No ad at launch and none when you come back to the app.
  • Your first 10 runs are ad-free; after that, at most one ad every 5 runs.
  • A new player starts flying at once, straight into level 1-1.
  • Bigger Mystery Box star prizes.
R2

Reflection

  • Ten night hours, Dusk to Dawn, each with its own horizon.
  • A lake that mirrors the sky, and a twin that lives in it.
  • All-new screens and type, and 26 skins redrawn.
  • Original music where every gate plays a note.
  • Dive: SOLO's comet now holds its breath under water.
R3

Voyage begins

  • A world map of hand-made levels you can finish.
  • World 1, Still Water: 12 levels and a secret.
  • Three moons per level, motes to catch, checkpoints.
  • Stars for every gate and a bonus for each first clear.
R4

Four new worlds

  • Eclipse, Tides, Pulse and Shattered Mirror.
  • SOLO levels inside Voyage, with their own air to manage.
  • 17 new skins: 8 creatures in the shop, 9 earned only in Voyage.
  • The Guide, two checkpoints on long levels, and a redrawn map.
Voyage

65 levels, every one proven

Five worlds of 12 levels each, plus one secret level per world. Every level is fixed and hand-made, and every one is checked by a solver before it ships: it can be finished, and all three moons can be won in a single run.

Three moons a level

Moons are never taken away once won.

Finish the levelCatch all 3 motesFinish without a checkpoint

Checkpoints

Longer levels have a checkpoint, and the longest have two. Crash, and FROM CHECKPOINT puts you back at the furthest one you crossed, free and as often as you like.

The Guide

Stuck? A guide draws a path through the level that is proven to clear it. You start with 2 and earn one more for every 6 levels you clear; more cost 10 moons each.

Secrets and rewards

Clear a world's level 12 while holding 24 of its 36 moons and its secret level opens. First clears pay 5,240 stars across Voyage, and nine skins can only be earned here.

New players begin in Voyage. Endless TWINS and SOLO open once World 1 is cleared; 2 Players, VS Computer, Online and challenges are open from the start.

The night sky

Ten hours from Dusk to Dawn

In Endless, every 20 gates the hour changes. The sky, the horizon and the music change with it, and the next hour's landscape slides in a few gates before it arrives.

The lake

Only the sky reflects

The water mirrors the sky, the horizon and the planet. The gates and the comets never reflect, so the twin is the only thing down there that moves with you.

Sound

Your flight plays the music

Every gate you pass plays the next note of the hour's melody over its own pad. A lower octave joins at 40 and a higher voice at 100. In Voyage, Worlds 2 to 5 each bring their own sound.

Dive · SOLO

Hold your breath

Below the waterline the SOLO comet breathes from a 7.5 second tank, and it refills fast in the air. The course never forces you under for longer than about 3.5 seconds.

Ways to play

Endless, together, or the course of the day

Endless

TWINS

The original: the star above, the reflection below, mirrored every frame. Thread both through every gate.

Endless

SOLO

One comet with the whole screen to itself, one opening per gate, openings that change size as you climb, and an oxygen tank under the water.

Daily

Course of the Day

One course a day, the same for everyone in your mode. Finish it for 50 stars, beat par for 100 more.

Same phone

2 Players

Top half against bottom half on one device. First to crash loses.

Offline

VS Computer

A computer opponent at one of eight skill levels, picked for each match.

With a friend

Online room

Host a private room and share its code, or join a friend's. Played peer to peer.

Asynchronous

Ghost Challenge

Send a friend your run as a code or a link. They fly the identical course and pickups, chasing your score.

Leaderboards

TWINS and SOLO best

Two boards on Google Play Games. Game Center joins on iPhone.

Missions

441 missions

Score milestones in both modes, VS Computer wins, power use and collection goals, each paying stars.

Endless powers

Pickups that help, and a few that don't

Powers float into TWINS and SOLO runs. Collect them, upgrade them in the Shop, or arm a booster before a run. No power ever adds points: one gate is always one point.

Collection

Earn stars, dress the twins

A star for every gate, in one wallet shared by every mode. Ten planets unlock by score. Character skins are bought with stars or earned, and each one has its own animation and trail.

Shards from the Mystery Box lower the price of their own skin: hold half the shards and pay half. Shards are never spent and never lost. Ten older skins are now Classics; they stay listed for players who own one or hold its shards.

What's in the Mystery Box?

A free box arrives every fifth TWINS or SOLO run. One tap rolls one prize. It is never sold, and nothing inside can be bought.

Bars are relative. The skin chance is 0.5% for each eligible skin you don't own, up to 4% (3% for a new player); any unused share rolls into Nothing (15% for a new player). After four empty boxes in a row, the next roll leans toward stars, and once a day an optional ad can upgrade a roll. Both only improve these odds.

Ads, plainly

Twinstar is free and ad-supported. These are the rules the code follows.

  • 1No ad when the app opens or when you return to it.
  • 2No ads in your first 10 TWINS or SOLO runs (a revived run counts as two). After that, at most one every 5 runs and never within 3 minutes of the last.
  • 3Video rewards only when you tap for one.
  • 4Voyage stays quiet: an ad can follow a cleared level, or a fourth crash in a row on the same level, under the same limits.
  • 5In an online room, both phones may show one ad together every third match.
