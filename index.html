The whole game in one thumb

Hold. Release. Survive.

No buttons, no menus to fight. Just one touch that bends two comets at once.

01

Hold to steer

Press anywhere to pull the comets toward the center axis. Let go and they drift back outward. That single input is your only control.

02

Mirror & thread

The two comets mirror each other across the axis. Asymmetric gates slide in from the right — guide both comets safely through every gap.

03

Shifting skies

Score a point for every gate you pass. Every 20 gates the entire color palette transforms, cycling through ten moods from Aurora to Indigo.

Equip · Upgrade

Cosmic Power-Ups

Collect in-run powers or buy pre-run boosters to control space, time, and gravity. Upgrade their levels in the Shop to extend their duration.

Shield

Absorbs a single collision with a gate block or wall. Your primary defense for breaking high scores.

Slow

Temporarily slows down the velocity of incoming gates, letting you plan your mirror drifts in slow-motion.

Speed

Accelerates comets and gates. Earn higher star multipliers and test your high-velocity reflexes.

Ghost

Phases comets into a transparent quantum state. Pass directly through solid blocks safely.

Stars-×2

Double the value of all stars collected while active, maximizing your wallet income for skin shopping.

Shrink

Compresses the size of both comets, making it easy to slip through narrow gaps and tight asymmetric gates.

Star Surge

Triggers a massive cosmic burst that spawns high-density stars all along your flight paths.

Gravity Invert

Inverts the steer/drift control mechanics. A mind-bending free anomaly spawned dynamically in-run.

Bars are relative. Skin odds (0.5% per cheaper skin you don't own yet, up to 4%) shrink as you collect them — any unused chance rolls into “Nothing”.
