The whole game in one thumb

Hold. Release. Survive.

No buttons, no menus to fight. Just one touch that bends two comets at once.

01

Hold to steer

Press anywhere to pull the comets toward the center axis. Let go and they drift back outward. That single input is your only control.

02

Mirror & thread

The two comets mirror each other across the axis. Asymmetric gates slide in from the right — guide both comets safely through every gap.

03

Shifting skies

Score a point for every gate you pass. Every 20 gates the entire color palette transforms, cycling through ten moods from Aurora to Indigo.
